# Prompts de las 2 landings

Pega estos prompts en tu herramienta de creación web. Están escritos para que la herramienta cree una landing de una sola pantalla, lista para capturar en escritorio.

## 1 · Spatial UI — ORBIT

```text
Create a premium, one-screen desktop landing page for “ORBIT”, a spatial audio app. The page must feel like a room for music rather than a flat website. Use a deep indigo-to-violet atmospheric background with a soft luminous centre, very subtle elliptical floor rings, and floating translucent glass cards arranged in believable 3D perspective. The hero copy on the lower left says “Music that has a place.” with the eyebrow “THE SPATIAL AUDIO APP”, a compact paragraph, and a white “Start exploring ↗” button. In the middle/right, create a sculptural interface: a main glass card called “Your next room”, a smaller “Now playing / Night Rituals” card, an “Explore” card, a glossy lavender-blue sphere, and a pink orbit ring. Use Manrope for type, elegant spacing, soft ambient shadows, thin luminous borders, and no stock photos. Include a minimal ORBIT logo and a sparse navigation. The result should look like an art-directed Apple Vision Pro-inspired editorial concept, not a generic dashboard. Make it responsive but optimise the first viewport for a 1600 × 1000 screenshot.
```

## 2 · Skeuomorphism — MAGNET

```text
Create a premium, one-screen desktop landing page for “MAGNET”, an analogue-inspired digital mixtape app. The page should feel tactile, mechanical and nostalgic without becoming kitsch. Use a charcoal studio background with subtle grain, warm ivory typography, a serif headline on the left that reads “Keep the good parts.”, an eyebrow “ANALOGUE MUSIC, DIGITALLY”, short supporting copy and a raised physical-looking “Build a mixtape ▶” button. On the right, create a large cassette-deck interface with brushed dark metal, bevelled edges, realistic inset shadows, a glowing green LCD display, a metal knob, mechanical playback buttons and a beautiful beige cassette with two visible reels. Use carefully controlled highlights and shadows to communicate materials. Include a compact MAGNET wordmark and minimal nav. Use Playfair Display for the headline and DM Mono for utility labels. No stock photography, no gradients that feel generic, no glass UI. The result should look like a beautifully photographed piece of vintage hi-fi translated into a refined website, optimised for a 1600 × 1000 screenshot.
```
